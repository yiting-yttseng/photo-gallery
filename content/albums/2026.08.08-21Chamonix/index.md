**Aug 8–21, 2026 · Chamonix-Mont-Blanc (夏慕尼), France**

Thirteen days in the heart of the Alps — glaciers, via ferrata, a cross-border cycling trip, and the heights of Mont Blanc from both French and Italian sides.

---

## Day 1 · Aug 8 — Arrival (抵達夏慕尼)

Chamonix sits at the end of a deep valley carved by glaciers, surrounded by granite needles and the massive bulk of Mont Blanc. The town hums with climbers, hikers, and paragliders from around the world. A day to settle in, look up, and realize the scale of everything here is larger than photographs suggest.

---

## Day 2 · Aug 9 — North Balcony & Mer de Glace (北邊陽台 & 冰之海)

**Route:** Grand Balcon Nord — Plan de l'Aiguille → Montenvers
**Actual:** 12.43 km · 5h 22m · ↑170m ↓550m

The Balcon Nord traverses high above the Chamonix valley with unobstructed views of the Mont Blanc massif. Starting from the Aiguille du Midi cable car mid-station at 2,310m, the path curves through boulder fields and snowfields before descending through forest to Montenvers. From there, a cable car brings you down to the Mer de Glace — the largest glacier in France — where iron ladders bolted into the rock mark how far the ice has retreated in recent decades.

## Day 3 · Aug 10 — Glacier Hiking, Italy Side (冰川健行，義大利側)

**Route:** Pointe Helbronner → Aiguilles Marbrees
**Actual:** ~3.12 km · 2h 37m (partial recording) · Altitude: 3,300–3,466m · Hard

An early start for the Skyway Monte Bianco cable car from Courmayeur, ascending to Pointe Helbronner at 3,466m. Up here the terrain is permanent snow and exposed rock. The crossing to Aiguilles Marbrees involves sections rated as technically hazardous — short, but requiring full attention. Looking south: the Italian valley. Looking north: the full south face of Mont Blanc.

## Day 4 · Aug 11 — Lac Blanc & Two Lakes Hike (白湖健行，兩湖健行)

**Route:** Aiguilles Rouges → Lac Blanc → Lacs des Chéserys → Argentière
**Actual:** 8.52 km · 4h 59m (partial recording) · ↑130m ↓1,140m · Hard

The classic view of Mont Blanc — reflected in the clear alpine water of Lac Blanc with the Aiguilles Rouges in the foreground. The trail starts high and drops through a series of glacial lakes before a long descent to Argentière. The full span of the Chamonix valley and its peaks visible the entire way down.

## Day 5 · Aug 12 — Grand Balcon Sud (南陽台)

**Route:** Le Brévent → Grand Balcon Sud → La Flégère
**Actual:** 12.03 km · 5h 58m (with scenic detour) · ↑110m ↓710m · Hard

The south-facing counterpart to the Balcon Nord. The trail runs beneath the Aiguilles Rouges, looking across to the entire Mont Blanc massif. A longer day than planned, with a detour for views that added several kilometers to the route.

## Day 6 · Aug 13 — Via Ferrata des Evettes (鐵索攀岩)

**Route:** Via Ferrata des Evettes
**Actual:** 3.19 km · 2h 35m (two segments merged, partial recording) · ↑220m ↓210m · Hard

Clipping into iron rungs and cables bolted directly into vertical rock. Via ferrata puts you in positions that would otherwise require full climbing gear — exposed ledges, overhanging sections, several hundred meters of air beneath your feet. The mental game is harder than the physical one.

## Day 7 · Aug 14 — Tramway du Mont-Blanc & Glacier de Bionnassay (TMB 齒軌小火車 & 比昂納塞冰川)

**Route:** Tramway du Mont-Blanc terminus → Glacier de Bionnassay
**Komoot estimate:** 854m · 18 min

The Tramway du Mont-Blanc — the highest rack railway in France, built in 1908 — winds from Saint-Gervais up to its terminus at Nid d'Aigle (2,372m). From there, a short walk reaches the Bionnassay Glacier. A slower, quieter day in the massif.

## Day 8 · Aug 15 — La Jonction: Glacier des Bossons & Glacier de Taconnaz (博松冰川 & 塔科納冰川)

**Route:** Chalet du Glacier des Bossons → La Jonction
**Actual:** 17.00 km · 8h 13m · ↑1,140m ↓1,150m · Hard

The longest and hardest day. La Jonction is a moraine ridge at 2,589m where the Bossons and Taconnaz glaciers converge — reached by hours of climbing through boulder fields and unstable glacial debris, with the Bossons serac towers looming overhead. The seracs shift and calve: you can hear them. The views from La Jonction are unobstructed in every direction.

## Day 10 · Aug 17 — Skyway Monte Bianco (義大利 Skyway 空中纜車)

A day above 3,000m without a rope. The Skyway Monte Bianco rises from Courmayeur to Pointe Helbronner at 3,466m in rotating panoramic gondolas. The deck looks directly onto the south face of Mont Blanc. On clear days the Matterhorn, Monte Rosa, and Gran Paradiso are all visible.

## Day 11 · Aug 18 — Cycling to Rifugio Elena (騎腳踏車穿越義大利)

**Route:** Courmayeur → Val Ferret → Rifugio Elena, Aosta Valley
**Actual:** 52.30 km · 6h 35m · ↑1,530m ↓1,530m · Hard

Trading boots for pedals. A 52km ride through the Val Ferret on the Italian side, finishing at Rifugio Elena — a mountain hut at 2,060m at the foot of the Grandes Jorasses, one of the most demanding north faces in the Alps. The last climb to the hut is steep. The view from the terrace is worth it.

## Day 12 · Aug 19 — Rifugio Bonatti (博納提山屋)

**Route:** Bivouac Rifugio Bonatti → Glacier du Petit Mont Gruettaz
**Actual:** 7.04 km · 2h 09m · ↑420m ↓360m · Moderate

Named after Walter Bonatti, the Italian alpinist who opened some of the most demanding routes in the Alps. The refuge sits above Val Ferret with a direct view of the Grandes Jorasses and its hanging glaciers. A final day of hiking at a quieter pace, watching clouds move through the peaks.
