Marathon Record (馬拉松紀錄) 2013–2026

2026/10/25 — Completed the EMC (完成 EMC)

European Marathon Classics (EMC)

Starting in the second half of 2026, Europe introduced the European Marathon Classics (EMC) medal: finish 5 of the 8 selected marathons and you earn a collector's medal combining all five races! Past results count too. Luckily, I had already run three of them without knowing about the programme — Vienna (2023), Rome (2024) and Madrid (2025) — so this year I added Warsaw in September and Frankfurt in October. Frankfurt started cold and rainy, with my shoes soaked before the start, but the weather cleared up around midday and I finished happily 🥳

The medal can currently only be collected in person after a race. Across the three marathons in the second half of 2026, 140 medals have been issued worldwide — one of them to Taiwan ☺️ I finished near the back of the pack every time, but always made it back before the cut-off 🤭 I'm so happy to have earned such a beautiful magnet medal so soon 🥰

Running in Europe is a real joy. I hope to save up enough to bring home the World Marathon Majors (WMM) medals too 🤑 So far I only have Berlin; I keep entering the ballot for London but haven't been drawn 🥲

歐洲從 2026 下半年開始發 European Marathon Classic (EMC) 獎牌，也就是在 8 場選定的賽事中完成 5 場，就能拿到這 5 場馬拉松的集合獎牌！過往的參賽紀錄也可以計入，很幸運的之前在完全不知道有這個活動的情況下已經跑過 3 場（2023 維也納、2024 羅馬、2025 馬德里），今年為了拿到獎牌又再跑了 9 月的華沙和 10 月的法蘭克福馬拉松。雖然法蘭克福的早晨又冷又下雨，還沒起跑鞋子已全濕，但在中午天氣好轉後還是順利完賽🥳

獎牌目前只能在賽後親自領取，2026 年下半年的三場馬拉松總共發出了全球 140 面獎牌，台灣目前發出 1 面☺️ 雖然每一場成績都吊車尾，但也都穩定的在關門前跑回來🤭 很高興自己可以那麼快拿到那麼美的磁鐵獎牌🥰

歐洲跑步真的很愉快，希望以後存到很多錢錢，也可以把世界馬拉松 WMM 的獎牌帶回家🤑（目前只有柏林 1 馬，想抽倫敦但一直抽不到🥲）
