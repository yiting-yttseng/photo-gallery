Dresden was my base during a research stay in Germany — a city known for its Baroque architecture, the Elbe river valley, and easy day trips into the Saxon-Bohemian borderlands.

---

## Schwebebahn Dresden (德勒斯登懸掛式空中纜車)

One of the world's oldest suspension railways, running above the Loschwitz valley since 1901.
